Privacy Policy

Noël Tier 1 Hub · Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version: Noël Tier 1 Hub collects nothing. There's no account, no tracking, no ads and no servers. Everything you type stays on your phone, encrypted.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It doesn't connect to the internet to work.

Where your information lives

Your lists, gift ideas, budgets, guest names, notes and settings are saved only on your device. They're encrypted with AES-256 before they're stored, and the encryption key is kept in your phone's secure keychain. We (the maker of Noël Tier 1 Hub) can't see, access or recover any of it.

Things you choose to share

  • Cards and invites: when you share a video or picture, it's made on your phone and passed to the app you pick (Messages, Mail and so on). We never receive a copy.
  • Messages and email: when you text or email a guest from the app, your own Messages or Mail app opens. We never see those messages.
  • Backups: a backup file is created only when you tap Save a backup, and goes wherever you choose to save it.
  • Feedback: if you choose to email us an idea, we receive only what you send and use it only to reply and improve the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only for your timers and weekly reminders, which are scheduled on your phone.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: optional, for the app lock. This is handled by your phone. The app never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • Photos (add only): used only if you choose to save a card or invite to your Photos.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and everything it saved on your phone is gone.
